Scope

One Chart is a private publishing tool operated by a single individual for their own use. It is not a product offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no user accounts, and no means for anyone other than the operator to use it. These terms exist because a service integration requires them, and they describe an application with exactly one user.

What the application does

It generates short data-visualisation videos from publicly published statistics (principally the World Bank and Our World in Data), and publishes them to the operator's own social media accounts. Every video states its data source on screen and in its description.

Use of connected platforms

The application accesses connected platforms only through their official APIs, using credentials the operator has granted for their own accounts, and only to publish the operator's own original content and read back that content's performance figures. It does not act on behalf of any other person or account, and it does not attempt to circumvent any platform limit, rate limit or policy.

All published videos are produced by the operator from public data. No third-party video, image or audio is republished, and no content is uploaded on anyone else's behalf. Responsibility for published content rests with the operator.
