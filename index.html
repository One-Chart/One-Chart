One Chart

Short data-visualisation videos, built from public statistics.

One Chart turns published statistics into short animated charts — line charts, bar-chart races and ranking countdowns — and posts them to its own social accounts. Every video names its data source on screen and in the description.

Data comes from the World Bank Open Data and Our World in Data.
